# Jewelry E-Commerce Store – Version 2.0

## Overview
Version 2.0 provides an enhanced online jewelry shopping experience
with online payment, order tracking, customer reviews, and promotional
features.

## Features from Version 1.0
- Browse jewelry products.
- View product details.
- Shopping cart.
- Order placement.
- Delivery information.
- Order confirmation.

## Features from Version 1.1
- Customer registration and login.
- Customer profile.
- Product search.
- Product category filters.
- Price filters.
- Wishlist.
- Previous order history.

## New Features in Version 2.0

### Online Payment
- Customers can select an online payment method.
- Customers can pay for their orders online.
- The system displays payment confirmation.
- The system maintains payment status for orders.

### Order Tracking
- Customers can view their order status.
- Orders can have statuses such as:
  - Order Placed
  - Processing
  - Shipped
  - Delivered

### Product Reviews
- Customers can provide ratings for purchased jewelry.
- Customers can write reviews about products.
- Product pages display customer ratings and reviews.

### Offers and Discounts
- The system supports promotional offers.
- Discount information can be displayed for eligible products.
- The shopping cart displays applicable discounts.

### Reports
- The system can maintain basic sales information.
- Reports can include:
  - Total orders
  - Sold products
  - Available products
  - Customer orders
  - Sales information

## Version Information
Version 2.0 extends the jewelry e-commerce platform with online
payments, order tracking, customer reviews, discounts, and reports.
